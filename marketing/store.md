# Store page texts (CrazyGames, itch.io)

**Title:** Clutchzone

**Short description (one line):**
A free cartoon tactical shooter in your browser: 4v4, plant the bomb or defuse it.

**Description:**
Clutchzone is a free cartoon tactical shooter that runs right in your browser — no download, no account.

Jump into a 4v4 match in seconds. Attackers carry the bomb to site A or B and plant it; defenders hold
the sites, take them back and defuse. Win 7 rounds to take the match. Bots fill the teams, so there is
always a game going.

- 3 maps: Outpost (desert town), Dockyard (harbour full of containers), Frostbite (snowy village)
- 3 weapons: the AR-7 rifle with a learnable spray, the P-9 pistol, the Longbow sniper with a scope
- Level up to unlock weapon paints, hats and kill effects that everyone sees
- Daily challenges and a bonus for every day you play in a row
- Play with friends: everyone in the same room

**Controls:**
WASD — move · Mouse — look and aim · Left click — shoot · Right click — scope (sniper) · R — reload ·
Space — jump · C — crouch · Shift — walk quietly · 1 / 2 / 3, Q, mouse wheel — switch weapons ·
E (hold) — plant or defuse the bomb · Tab — scoreboard · Esc — menu

**Genre / tags:** Shooter, FPS, Multiplayer, 3D, Cartoon, Team, Bomb, Sniper, Action, Io

**Platform:** desktop (mouse and keyboard). **Orientation:** landscape. **Language:** English.
**Multiplayer:** yes, up to 10 players per room (4 v 4 filled with bots). **Age:** PEGI 12 style cartoon violence, no blood gore.
